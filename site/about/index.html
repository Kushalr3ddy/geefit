About us

A new company, with an old idea about how industries grow.

GeeFit Innovations Private Limited was incorporated in Bengaluru in 2026 to do one thing well: build industrial expos that reach past the metro that usually hosts them. We are based in Jayanagar, and Global Expo Events is our first project.

We'd rather say that plainly than pretend to a history we don't have. What we do have is a defined programme, an anchor industry partner, and [N] years of the founders' experience in [field].

Being new is not a weakness if you name it first. It becomes one the moment a buyer discovers it for themselves.

Our inspiration

What we've set ourselves to be judged by.

Sir M. Visvesvaraya built dams, cities and colleges in Karnataka on the belief that industry is what turns engineering into livelihoods. That is the standard we've set ourselves: an expo is only worth holding if somebody leaves it with a contract, a job or a place on a course. We keep his portrait on our wall as a reminder that the measure is what got built, not what got announced.
What we're for

Three short statements.

Reach, not prestige

Seven cities instead of one flagship hall. The supplier who can't afford a Delhi trip is exactly who we're for.

Sectors that touch

Aerospace, finance, education and employment in one hall, because that's how a real supply chain gets staffed and funded.

Outcomes on record

We'll publish what each stop produced — leads, hires, enrolments — and be judged on it.
