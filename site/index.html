What Global Expo Events is

The opportunity travels to them.

Picture it. Most expos happen once — one hall, one city, one window that most of the country never gets to walk through.

We're changing that. Global Expo Events takes the entire floor on the road — the same exhibition, the same global delegations — across seven Indian cities in twenty-one non-stop days. The supplier in Coimbatore. The graduate in Kolkata. The investor in Mumbai.

Five sectors at every stop. Delegations from across the world. And a single mission: to connect Indian business, end to end, and open it to the planet.

Our inspiration

What we've set ourselves to be judged by.

Sir M. Visvesvaraya built dams, cities and colleges in Karnataka on the belief that industry is what turns engineering into livelihoods. That is the standard we've set ourselves: an expo is only worth holding if somebody leaves it with a contract, a job or a place on a course. We keep his portrait on our wall as a reminder that the measure is what got built, not what got announced.
