Sectors

Seven verticals. One floor.

Each written so a company in that sector recognises itself and knows why to book.

Aircraft manufacturing

Airframes, engines, avionics, tooling, MRO. The anchor floor: primes and tier-one suppliers alongside the small machining and composites shops trying to qualify into their supply chains. If you make a part that ends up on an aircraft, this is your hall.

Airlines

Carriers, ground handling, cabin interiors, catering, training and airport services — the operating side of aviation, meeting the people who build and maintain what it flies.

Aerospace & satellites

Launch services, satellite manufacturing, ground stations, earth observation and downstream data. India's fastest-moving industrial sector, and the one with the most new entrants who need a first showcase.

Planet research

Space science, planetary and atmospheric research, remote sensing and climate observation — institutes, universities and labs presenting work to the industry that could commercialise it, and to the students who might join it.

Finance

Banks, NBFCs, export credit, leasing, venture and private equity. Deep-tech manufacturing lives or dies on access to capital, so we put the lenders on the floor rather than on a panel.

Industry & manufacturing

Precision machining, castings, composites, electronics, automation, robotics, testing and industrial services — the broad base that supplies every sector above.

Technology

Software, AI, simulation, digital twins, sensors and cybersecurity applied to all of the above — the layer that increasingly decides who wins the contract.

Five tracks, running at every stop

The verticals are who exhibits. The tracks are what a visitor gets done.

A single visit can mean a course prospectus, a job interview, a distributor conversation and an investor meeting — in one afternoon.

Education expo

Engineering colleges, aviation academies and technical institutes, with counselling desks and admissions on the day.

Employment expo

Recruiters hiring on the floor — walk-in interviews, apprenticeships, and a CV bank open to every exhibitor.

Finance expo

Working capital, equipment finance, export credit and equity, with scheduled lender meetings for MSME exhibitors.

Industry expo

The main trade floor: machinery, components, materials and services, with buyer–supplier matchmaking.

Technology expo

Demonstrations and launches — automation, AI, simulation and the tooling that changes how the sector works.
