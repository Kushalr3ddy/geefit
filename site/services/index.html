Services

We produce expos, end to end.

Global Expo Events is ours. We also produce and manage events for industry bodies, institutions and companies who want their own — using the same venue relationships, delegate lists and build partners.

Five service blocks

What GeeFit does as a business.

Expo & trade show production

Concept, venue, floor plan, exhibitor sales, build, operations, teardown. One team accountable from the first site visit to the last invoice.

Multi-city tours

Our speciality: the same exhibition taken to several cities on one plan, so a brand covers regional markets without seven separate projects.

Career & education pavilions

Recruitment and admissions floors run inside a larger expo — booths, walk-in interview slots, session programming and candidate registration.

Sponsorship & brand activation

Packaging title, sector and track sponsorships, and making sure a sponsor's presence is felt on the floor and not just on the banner.

Event technology

Online registration and QR check-in at the door, a lead-capture app exhibitor staff use to scan and qualify visitors on the spot, and an exhibitor portal for floor plans, invoices and staff passes — one login that carries across all seven cities.
